### Game Details & Guide
Money Miner 2 Y8 Guide



Game Overview


The game of Money Miner 2 is a browser-based game where you play as a miner who must extract valuable minerals from asteroids while avoiding obstacles.



How to Play


In the game, you are presented with a series of asteroids that contain different amounts of minerals. You can mine these minerals by clicking on them and collecting them in your inventory.


The objective of the game is to accumulate the most valuable collection of minerals within a set amount of time or until you run out of resources.


Tips and Tricks for Success



• Make sure to keep an eye on your health and oxygen levels, as running out can result in game over.
• Use the right tools (e.g. pickaxe, shovel) for each type of asteroid to maximize efficiency.
• Focus on collecting valuable minerals and resources, but don't neglect your own needs.
• Be careful when navigating through dense asteroid fields.
• Manage your inventory wisely, as you may need to sell some items at a lower price than others.

Additional Features
